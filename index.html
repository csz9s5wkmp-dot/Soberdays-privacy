SoberDays Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

SoberDays helps you track habits you want to quit, your streaks, and (if you choose) estimated money saved. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information you enter

You may enter habit names or choose a preset, select an emoji, set a start date, and optionally enter an estimated amount spent per day. This information is stored locally on your device. SoberDays does not require an account.

Widgets and device storage

If you use a SoberDays widget, the app shares the habit information needed to display your streak through Apple’s on-device app group storage. Your tracking information is not sent to SoberDays servers. If you enable iCloud device backups, Apple may include app data in those backups under your Apple account settings.

Purchases

SoberDays Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Apple handles payment and purchase information under its own privacy policy. SoberDays receives purchase status from Apple so it can unlock Pro features; we do not receive your payment card details.

Exports

If you choose the Pro export feature, SoberDays creates a file from your tracking information and opens Apple’s share sheet. The file is shared only with the destination you select. That destination’s privacy practices then apply.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

SoberDays does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not sell or share your tracking information with us or third parties. Data leaves the app only when you choose to export it, or when Apple processes a purchase or an enabled device backup.

Retention and deletion

Your tracking information remains on your device until you remove a habit, clear the app’s data, or delete the app. Deleting the app may not remove information already included in an iCloud or device backup; manage backups through your Apple device settings.

Children’s privacy

SoberDays is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s practices change. The current version will be posted at this address with a revised effective date.
